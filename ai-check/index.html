What this can and can't catch

Blocked: images carrying Content Credentials or an IPTC "made by AI" label (ChatGPT / DALL·E, Adobe Firefly, Microsoft Designer, Google and others add these), images with Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, InvokeAI or NovelAI settings saved inside, and files naming a generator such as Midjourney in their details.

  • Not caught: an AI image whose labels were removed, for example a screenshot of it or a copy re-saved by some apps. Google's invisible SynthID watermark can't be read here either.
  • Photos taken with the identifier's own Take photo button are always accepted: they come straight from the camera.
  • "No AI labels found" is not proof that a photo is real. A photo with camera details (make, model, date taken) is more likely to be genuine.
